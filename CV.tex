%-----------RESEARCH STATEMENT-----------
\section{\textbf{Research Statement}}
\justify
My research focuses on AI for Science at the intersection of generative modeling, multimodal foundation models, and agentic reasoning for molecular discovery. My work spans deep graph generation, protein and molecule modeling, cross-modal learning between molecular structures and experimental data, and large-scale LLM training and reinforcement learning. My long-term goal is to build agentic multimodal foundation models that integrate graphs, spectra, proteins, natural language, and scientific tools to accelerate molecular and biological discovery.
