%-----------RESEARCH STATEMENT-----------
\section{\textbf{Research Statement}}
\justify
My research focuses on AI for Science at the intersection of generative modeling, multimodal foundation models, and agentic reasoning for molecular discovery. I also have a strong interest in applying large-scale machine learning to industrial recommendation and retrieval systems, particularly in leveraging LLMs for query understanding, content generation, and user intent modeling. My work spans both fundamental research (deep graph generation, cross-modal alignment between molecular structures and experimental data) and applied ML systems (production-grade training pipelines, reinforcement learning from human feedback, and scalable retrieval infrastructure). My long-term goal is to build agentic multimodal foundation models that integrate heterogeneous data sources---graphs, spectra, proteins, natural language, and behavioral signals---with tool use and closed-loop reasoning to accelerate discovery in science and improve user experiences at scale.
